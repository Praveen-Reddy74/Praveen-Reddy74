## About Me

4.5+ years across enterprise BI delivery (Accenture), ERP operations (Cognizant), and AI-driven project management (MAQ Software), now extending that background into hands-on Generative AI, Agentic AI, and Process Intelligence work. I design Power BI dashboards and enterprise systems the same way I design an agent workflow or a RAG pipeline — starting from the business question, not the tool.

My current focus is the path from **descriptive analytics → predictive modelling → generative & agentic AI → process intelligence**, applied to real business problems: claims analysis, operations coordination, schema migration, and process adherence.
